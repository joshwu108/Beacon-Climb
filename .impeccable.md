## Design Context

Derived from the BC mobile brief (2026-10-20), `docs/ux/design-tokens.md` (BC-0008) and the PRD. The owner asked
for a cream theme and a deliberate mobile direction; everything else below follows the existing web tokens.

### Users
Board climbers in a gym, between attempts: phone in one chalky hand, standing under a training board
(the synthetic demo board `synthetic-demo-1`). Jobs: pick what to climb next, see the holds, log what
happened, and understand *why* Beacon suggested a route. Demoed on a laptop in a phone-sized frame.

### Brand Personality
Field-guide calm. Three words: **chalk-dusted, methodical, candid**. Beacon explains, never hypes; no
"AI sparkle", no predictions about the person's body or outcomes.

### Aesthetic Direction
"Topo Field Guide, pocket edition": a guidebook printed on cream uncoated paper. Ink is the primary action
colour; beacon teal is reserved for recommendations only. Hard offset paper shadows (no blur), square
stamps, round sheets. The board diagram is the hero. Display face Young Serif (grade numerals, route names),
UI face Atkinson Hyperlegible Next. Light theme is cream paper; dark theme is "lamp-lit crag" (espresso
paper, cream ink). Anti-references: SaaS dashboards, glassmorphism, neon-on-dark, gradient text.

### Design Principles
1. The board is the hero: the biggest surface and the only layered depth on a screen.
2. Teal means "Beacon suggested this", and nothing else.
3. Shape and text carry meaning; colour only reinforces (hold roles, states, freshness).
4. One ink primary action per screen; touch targets at least 44 pt.
5. Motion explains a state change (lineup arrives, sheet opens) and is skipped under reduced motion.
